About Me

Tarek Eissa is a passionate and dedicated robotics enthusiast. Especially, he is interested in Multi-Robot System, Aerial Robotics and Dexterous Manipulation using ROS1 and ROS2 architecture including the whole process in between: mechanical design, computer vision, control, software implementation, and hardware deployment.
