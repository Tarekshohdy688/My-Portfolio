About Me

Tarek Eissa is a passionate and dedicated robotics enthusiast. Especially, he is interested in Multi-Robot System, Aerial Robotics and Dexterous Manipulation using ROS architecture including the whole process in between: mechanical design, computer vision, control, software implementation, and hardware deployment. Here is a glimse of the work he has done so far during his academic and freelancing journey categorized according to its relevance in a chronological order.
